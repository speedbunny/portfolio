Project Insight: Consolidated Dashboard

A synthesis of key findings and strategic recommendations from the project deliverables.

The Original Client Ask

The 'FactFind' questionnaire, while comprehensive, experienced significant customer drop-off. The primary objective was to analyse the 326-question process to identify and eliminate friction. The core strategic question was: "How can we shorten the questionnaire to increase completion rates without materially impacting business process accuracy?"

Definitive Strategic Recommendation

The comprehensive analysis across all project phases provides a clear, data-validated path forward. The optimal strategy is a two-phased approach that maximises efficiency with minimal risk and operational complexity.

Execute a Population-Level Optimisation immediately, followed by an Adaptive Routing implementation based on product interest.

Conclusively abandon the demographic-specific routing approach as it is operationally complex and statistically unjustified.

Visualising the Core Findings

Friction is Universal

The analysis in DEL 4, confirmed by DEL 9, shows that the most problematic questions create friction for all customers, regardless of age or income. This is the foundation of the population-level strategy.

  • ✗
    Financial Scrutiny: Detailed questions about granular spending (e.g., clothing, food) feel invasive.
  • ✗
    Complex Calculations: Questions requiring long-term projections or complex maths are frequently abandoned.
  • ✗
    Pricing & Premiums: Asking for premium amounts early triggers price sensitivity and drop-offs.
  • ✗
    Sensitive Medical Details: Detailed requests about specific treatments cause significant resistance.

Valuable Demographic Insights (for Communication, Not Routing)

While not strong enough to justify separate journeys, the demographic analysis in DEL 9 uncovered key behavioural nuances. These are highly valuable for tailoring marketing and communication strategies.

Surprising Finding: High-Income Efficiency

Customers with incomes over £150k are less likely than the upper-middle income group to complete granular financial details. They value an efficient, relevant process over a comprehensive one. Implication: Market to this group by emphasising speed and relevance.

Confirmed Hypothesis: Middle-Age Peak

The 36-50 age group shows the highest and most consistent engagement across all protection and financial planning categories. Implication: This group is the core audience for comprehensive family protection messaging.

Insight: Young Adult Pragmatism

Contrary to assumptions, adults aged 18-35 complete medical questions at near-average rates, showing practical risk awareness despite high resistance to financial scrutiny. Implication: Communicate with transparency and focus on digital-native processes.

Insight: Lower-Income Focus on Essentials

The lower-income segment shows strong engagement with essential protection questions, despite being sensitive to cost. Implication: Lead with the value and affordability of core, essential cover.
